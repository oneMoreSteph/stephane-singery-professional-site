# NASA imagery used in the portfolio

- `blue-marble-2048.png` is NASA Earth imagery used for the interactive globe.
- `iss-render.png` and `sentinel-6-render.png` come from NASA's [Spacecraft Icons collection](https://science.nasa.gov/multimedia/spacecraft-icons/). They appear as detailed, transparent spacecraft renderings on the experience map.
- `juice-render.png` comes from the same NASA collection and represents ESA's Juice spacecraft on the map.
- `iss-photograph.jpg` is the actual [International Space Station photographed during a Crew Dragon flyaround](https://www.nasa.gov/international-space-station/space-station-gallery/). It appears in the high-stakes case-studies close view.
- `iss-b.glb` and `sentinel-6.glb` are earlier NASA 3D assets, no longer loaded by the site. They are kept in `archive/web/assets/nasa/`.

The map is an editorial navigation scene. Spacecraft sizes and orbital positions are illustrative; the imagery does not represent employment on these missions.
